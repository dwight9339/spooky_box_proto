# PAM8302 gain trim on the speaker monitor image

Date: 2026-10-09 (local, UTC-6; Spooky Bench log timestamps are UTC)  
Beads issue: none (operator hardware adjustment)

The operator wanted to set the gain trim pot on the PAM8302 breakout while audio
played through the speaker. The UI board was disconnected for access to the
backplane. The amplifier wiring is as in the
[speaker monitor experiment](2026-10-09-speaker-monitor-experiment.md): J5 line out
to A+/A−, power from `VSYS_RAW`, SD on `AMP_SD` (PG8). The operator confirmed the
board was connected and free.

## Silent speaker before the flash

The speaker was silent with the board powered. The board still held the
`RadioTuneQual` image `54w61-retune-20261009a`, flashed at 20:55 UTC for the retune
qualification. That preset inherits Debug, with `SPOOKY_SPEAKER_MONITOR=OFF`, so
line out stays powered down and PG8 holds the amplifier in shutdown. No wiring
fault was involved.

## Image

Built from `2cdeb1c` (clean tree) with GNU Tools for STM32 14.3.1
(14.3.rel1.20251027-0700), `SpeakerMonitor` preset,
`SPOOKY_BUILD_ID=speaker-trim-20261009a`.

| Core | SHA-256 |
| --- | --- |
| CM7 | `39eddd0f35f6be94f63491cfd4e8e2a2fe53174e3a626236320821d3c9d2cad6` |
| CM4 | `bb908efa2653115ca98dfa471075136edb03cfdcd6e03c3adfe04c5fd8451799` |

The first flash attempt stopped before programming with `firmware_identity`. The
`build/SpeakerMonitor` cache still held `SPOOKY_BUILD_ID=jr0-speaker-20261009b` from
the earlier experiment, so the image did not match the new manifest. A rebuild with
the new ID flashed at 02:22 UTC (2026-10-10) and passed, with both cores verified
and the CM7 running.

## Operator result

**Result:** pass. The speaker played after the flash. The operator turned the
PAM8302 gain trim pot to its maximum and left it there, and noticed nothing out of
order.

## Not covered

- Clipping at maximum gain: the speaker carried mostly noise from a weak station, not
  loud program material, so distortion near the top of the shield pot was not
  judged on strong audio.
- Mute pop: the pop at the mute/unmute boundary and at band switches was not
  listened for again at the new gain.
- Supply voltage: the battery read 4171 mV, 100% charge. Behavior at lower
  `VSYS_RAW` was not checked.
- Recording: no session ran.
